AI Reading Partners for Young Learners

Shared reading is one of the richest ways young children build language and literacy, especially when an adult asks questions and responds to the child's ideas during a story. Yet this kind of dialogic reading is hard to provide consistently, at home or in the classroom.

This project designs AI reading partners powered by large language models. One strand builds bilingual conversational agents that read with children inside interactive e-books. The other, StoryLab, helps K–2 teachers generate personalized stories for each student while keeping teachers in control.

Highlights

Bilingual conversational agents for dialogic reading

  • Led the project at Penn State as principal investigator: wrote the research proposal, built a working prototype, and trained a team of 20 graduate and undergraduate students for data collection and coding.
  • Named a finalist in the Nittany AI Challenge.
  • Ran a randomized controlled trial with 67 children aged 5–8 learning English as a foreign language, comparing AI-guided and parent-led dialogic reading.
  • Gathered perspectives from children, parents, and educators to inform the agent's design.
  • Continued this line of work as a visiting scholar at Harvard's Child-Centered AI Lab and BRIDGES Lab, on a conversational agent for children's video viewing and digital reading piloted with more than 20 families.

StoryLab: teacher-guided story generation

  • A multimodal system for K–2 teachers and students that combines students' personal interests with teacher-defined learning objectives.
  • Generates complete learning materials: story text, illustrations, vocabulary support, and a consistent narrative voice.
  • Keeps teachers in the loop to support pedagogical alignment and trust.

Findings

Bilingual conversational agents for dialogic reading

  • Children reading with the AI agent showed higher behavioral engagement and visual attention; children reading with a parent showed more affective engagement and more talk about the story.
  • The bilingual agent supported children's reading comprehension and vocabulary comparably to parent-led reading.
  • Children, parents, and educators expressed strong support for the agent's educational value.
  • In a design-probe study, the agent supported children's language production, encouraged interactive reading, and sustained their engagement.

StoryLab: teacher-guided story generation

  • Evaluations demonstrated StoryLab's effectiveness and usability, positioning it as a promising and scalable tool for personalized literacy instruction.
