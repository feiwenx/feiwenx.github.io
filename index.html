Feiwen Xiao

Ph.D. Candidate · University of Pennsylvania

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Learning Sciences and Technologies at the University of Pennsylvania Graduate School of Education, working with Dr. Shiyan Jiang. My research investigates how children learn with, about, and through AI. I design and study AI-powered learning experiences, such as LLM-powered reading companions, social robots, and AI literacy curricula, that support literacy, creativity, agency, and collaboration. My research interests include AI literacy, human-AI interaction, and embodied AI.

I'm open to collaborations on child-centered AI, AI literacy, and AI for children's learning. Feel free to reach out at feiwenx@upenn.edu.
