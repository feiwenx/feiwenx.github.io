Ph.D. Student · University of Pennsylvania

I am a Ph.D. student in Learning Sciences and Technologies at the University of Pennsylvania, working with Dr. Shiyan Jiang. My research investigates how children learn with, about, and through AI. I design and study AI-powered learning experiences that support literacy, creativity, agency, and collaboration. My research interests include AI literacy, human-AI interaction, and embodied AI.
